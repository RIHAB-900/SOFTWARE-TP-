Final conclusion

At first, we measured according to patients at high risk. The rate of high-risk patients who received AVX was high (47.3%), which is why the rate of hospitalized patients remained high (30.9%). Meanwhile, the rate of high-risk patients receiving standard care was low (3.1%), which is why the rate of hospitalized patients remained low (15.7%). This means that the initial measurement was not balanced, and therefore, the comparison was not fair.

In the second table, we measured according to initial risk, and the groups were similar. Therefore, the results made sense. When we assessed baseline risk across the three levels (low, moderate, and high), the lower rate was observed among patients treated with AVX-7 (2.6% , 16.5% , 51.3%), while the higher rate was observed among those receiving standard care(9.1% , 29.1% , 68.6%).

And this is called a “Simpson’s paradox comparison” we cannot say the AVX-7 is the cause because the study is observation . So, the comparison between the two tables was not similar
